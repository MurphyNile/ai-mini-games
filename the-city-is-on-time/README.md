# The City Is On Time — One More Second

A two-minute browser game by Murphy Nile, part of the **AI-Assisted Mini Games** series.

## Play

Download [the HTML file](index.html) and open it in a browser. Click **Start**, then hold the green pedestrian signal (or press and hold **Space**) to extend the crossing by up to four seconds per cycle. The signal countdown pauses while the city clock keeps running.

## Research practice

This study asks who gets extra time, and who has to wait for it. Holding the light can help a slower walker cross and delay a delivery van. The interface decides whose need to show, and the final score counts the walkers you helped only if they arrived on time.

The project extends my work on how technical systems guide attention and action. In *Control Club*, timing and screen changes direct where viewers look. Here, a countdown and a score shape when players choose to help.

AI assisted the prototyping of the game. The offline build runs on fixed simulation rules and needs no account, installation, or network connection.
