# Pixel Kitten · Meow to the Beat

A rhythm game about attention and practice.

## Play

Download [the English HTML file](Pixel_Kitten_EN.html) and open it in a browser. Listen to the kittens and repeat their rhythm. In Freestyle, you can make your own tune without a score.

## Research practice

The game asks players to listen closely, hold a rhythm in mind, and play it back. After a Good result, players can move on or try the same rhythm again. That choice gives them room to keep practicing after they have already passed.
